Ada Sparks Studio

A desktop creator app for producing and publishing original, fact-checked maker videos — with honest AI disclosure — to TikTok, using TikTok’s official APIs only.

Finish connecting TikTok

Copy this address and paste it into Ada Sparks Studio → Settings → TikTok.

What it is

Ada Sparks Studio runs on the creator’s own computer. It helps a creator write, fact-check, render and post short educational videos about making, electronics and code. Videos are original. They are labelled as AI-generated in the caption (including #AIgenerated) and, when TikTok’s Direct Post API is used, with TikTok’s official AI-generated content flag.

The Studio talks only to official platform APIs. It does not scrape TikTok, drive a browser, or post other people’s content.

Posting to TikTok

  • The creator signs in with TikTok Login Kit and grants only the scopes the app needs (user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload).
  • Before a Direct Post, the Studio shows the creator’s TikTok nickname, a preview, a privacy selector with no pre-selected default, interaction and commercial-disclosure controls (off by default), and the required consent text for TikTok’s Music Usage Confirmation.
  • Until TikTok approves the Content Posting API audit, videos can be sent to the creator’s TikTok inbox as drafts. The creator opens TikTok, chooses visibility (for example Everyone), turns on the AI-generated label, and taps Post.

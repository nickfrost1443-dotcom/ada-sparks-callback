Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026. This policy is written for users in the United Kingdom and the rest of the world, and is intended to meet UK GDPR transparency duties.

This Privacy Policy explains how Ada Sparks Studio (the “Studio”), operated by Nick Frost, United Kingdom (the “operator”, “we”), handles information when you use the Studio or this website. The Terms of Service also apply.

1. Who is responsible

Nick Frost is the data controller for personal data processed in connection with this website and, where the Studio is operated for others, for that operation. When you use the Studio only on your own computer to publish to your own accounts, much of the data stays on that computer under your control. TikTok is an independent controller of the data you send to TikTok.

2. Information we process

  • Account connection data. If you connect TikTok, the Studio stores on your computer the client key and secret you entered, your redirect URI, and OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, plus the scopes granted (for example user.info.basic, video.publish, video.upload).
  • Creator profile data from TikTok. The Studio calls TikTok’s official creator-info endpoint and displays your nickname, and may display your username and avatar, together with the privacy and interaction options TikTok returns for your account.
  • Content you create. Scripts, fact-check notes, video files, captions, post settings (privacy, comments, Duet, Stitch, commercial disclosure, AI-generated flags) and post identifiers returned by TikTok are stored in the Studio’s local folders on your computer.
  • Optional third-party keys. If you paste API keys for script or voice services (for example Google Gemini or Groq), those keys are stored locally and used only to call those services on your behalf.
  • This website. This is a static GitHub Pages site. It does not run a server-side account database. If you arrive here after a TikTok login, the page may show the full redirect address (which can include a one-time OAuth code) in your browser so you can paste it into the Studio. That address is not stored by us on a server. We do not set advertising cookies.

3. Why we use it

We use this information to operate the Studio, to connect and keep a TikTok login working, to show a compliant posting screen, to upload videos you choose to TikTok’s official APIs, and to keep a local record of what was posted. The legal bases under UK GDPR are: performing a contract with you (providing the Studio), and legitimate interests in running a secure creator tool. Optional cloud API keys are used only if you choose to provide them.

4. Sharing

When you post or connect an account, the Studio sends the necessary tokens, video file, caption and disclosure flags to TikTok using official APIs. If you enable a cloud writing or voice service, prompts or audio-related requests are sent to that provider. We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it for advertising. GitHub hosts this website (GitHub Pages) and may process standard connection logs under GitHub’s own terms.

5. Retention

Tokens, videos and settings remain on your computer until you disconnect TikTok, delete the files, or uninstall the Studio. You can disconnect TikTok in Settings, which clears the stored access and refresh tokens. This website does not keep a copy of your OAuth redirect.

6. Security

The Studio is designed to run locally (bound to 127.0.0.1) on your computer. You are responsible for the physical and login security of that computer. Tokens and keys are stored in a local configuration file. Transmit redirect URLs carefully and do not share them.

7. Your rights

If UK GDPR applies to our processing of your personal data, you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to certain processing, and to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). To make a request, open an issue at github.com/nickfrost1443-dotcom/ada-sparks-callback. If the data lives only on your computer, you can view or delete it there, including by disconnecting TikTok in the Studio.

8. Children

The Studio is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: github.com/nickfrost1443-dotcom/ada-sparks-callback.
